===============
Shelf

The shelf will be an empty rectangle with the edges being a 7mm dia
cylinder.  The corners will be filleted.  There will be a crossbar with
a lip where the Tray will sit.

The lip will not interfere with the finger scoops in the tray.

At the outside of the tray it should extend out to support the tray and
have small pegs that will sit in indents in the tray to lock it in
place.

===============
Base

The base is an "H" shape that will be hinged on the bottom of the shelf.
The base will have the outside and be rounded to match the bottom of the
shelf. The base legs will have a hole through and the shelf will have a
reduced diameter to become the pin for the hinge.

The base should fit entirely within the outside rectangle of the shelf
when folded.

The base will fold under the crossmember on the shelf, so both will be
47% of the thickness.  The base will take the lower, the shelf
crossmember the upper.

The base should have a crossmember that will be at the point the base
and leg come together.    The base crossmember will cross under the leg
and each will have 47% of the thickness, the base will take the lower
again, the leg the upper.

The base should have a raised place for the leg to click into when in
the upright position.   This should be the inverse of the leg's
crossmember so that crossmember clicks into it.

The base should be short enough to fit within the interior of the shelf
and not interfere with the leg crossmember.

===============
Leg

The leg will be hinged to the top of the shelf crossmember, it needs
about 120 degrees of movement from the flat folded down to meet the
base.  

The leg will have a crossmember at the end away from the hinge.  This
crossmember will be the cylinder profile of our shelf part. This
crossmember must also fit inside the space in the shelf part between the
crossmember and top.
